## Post 2 — Craft sample

**Image:** `02-script-sample.png`

```text
“She remembers the rain. He remembers her laugh. Neither remembers what they ordered.”

This fictional sample shows how a small, specific memory can do more work than a broad statement about love.

The opening gives an editor three immediate visual anchors: weather, expression and place. From there, the narration can move from a single remembered afternoon into the larger pattern of the relationship—without forcing the film into a speech that feels disconnected from the footage.

Our scripts are written for the edit, not just for the page. We account for:
• Spoken pace and breathing room
• Establishing shots and detail coverage
• Emotional turns
• The transition into vows, first look or final sequence

The example shown here is fictional and was created to demonstrate the VowScene format. No real couple or client story is represented.
```
