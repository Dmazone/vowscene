Questions

Before you hand over the files

Do guests need another account or app?

No. The mobile guide opens in a browser. The PDF can be printed or attached to existing messages.

Can you put door codes in the guide?

We do not place rotating door codes or reservation-specific personal data on public pages. Keep those details in your approved PMS or booking-channel message flow.

Is translation certified?

No. We provide guest-facing localization drafts and require the operator to approve all property facts and safety-critical wording. Certified legal translation is outside scope.

Will this guarantee fewer messages or more revenue?

No guarantee is made. The system is designed to make information easier to find and optional extras easier to understand. Operators should measure message volume and add-on sales before and after deployment.

What starts the three-day timeline?

The timeline starts after payment and receipt of complete property information, approved images and brand details.
