STRAIGHT ANSWERS

Know the boundary
before you buy.

A clear scope protects both sides and keeps delivery realistic.

Ask about your property
Do guests need another account or app?

No. The guide opens in a browser, and the PDF can be printed or attached to messages.

Can private door codes go in the guide?

Not on a public page. Keep rotating codes and reservation data in approved private messages.

Is translation included?

Packages include an English master. Additional languages are quoted separately and require operator approval.

Will this guarantee fewer messages or more revenue?

No. The system improves information delivery, but results depend on the property, guests and deployment.

What is not included?

PMS migration, booking management, live messaging, custom apps, printing, legal writing and certified translation.

When does the timeline begin?

After payment and receipt of complete property information, approved images and brand details.
