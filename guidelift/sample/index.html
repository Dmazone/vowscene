01 · Arrival

From the coastal road to the front door

Check-in

After 4:00 PM. Your reservation-specific access code is sent through the booking platform on arrival day.

Parking

Use the marked space beside the olive tree. One vehicle only. The turning area must remain clear.

Finding the house

At the blue ceramic sign, take the lower lane for 80 metres. Casa Marée is the second white gate.

This public demonstration intentionally contains no real address, access code or private guest information.

02 · Essentials

The answers guests look for first

Wi-Fi

Network: CasaMaree_Guest

Quiet hours

10:00 PM to 8:00 AM. No parties or unregistered visitors.

Climate

Close terrace doors while cooling is on. Set no lower than 22°C.

Water

Tap water is safe to drink. Filtered water is available in the refrigerator.

Waste

General waste under the sink. Recycling bins are beside the parking space.

Support

For urgent property issues, use the contact saved in your booking thread.

04 · Local rhythm

A short list, not a directory

Morning

Walk the lower coastal path before 9:00, then stop at the fictional Harbour Bakery.

Swim

The sheltered fictional Cala Verde beach is a 12-minute walk downhill.

Dinner

Reserve the fictional Terra & Salt terrace on weekends. Sunset tables go first.

05 · Checkout

A clear goodbye

  1. Check out by 10:00 AM.
  2. Place used towels in the bathroom basket.
  3. Load and start the dishwasher.
  4. Switch off lights and climate control.
  5. Close windows and lock the terrace door.
  6. Send a quick message through the booking platform when you leave.
