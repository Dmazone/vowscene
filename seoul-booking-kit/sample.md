# Seoul Visitor Booking Kit — sample

## Hair colour consultation

**English**

Please send one or two reference photos before booking. Your stylist will check your current colour, hair condition and the result you want before recommending a service. The final price depends on hair length, thickness and the amount of colour correction required. We will confirm the estimate before starting.

**日本語**

ご予約前に、希望する仕上がりの写真を1〜2枚お送りください。担当スタイリストが現在の髪色、髪の状態、希望の仕上がりを確認して、適したメニューをご案内します。料金は髪の長さ・量・カラー修正の内容によって変わります。施術前に見積もりを確認していただきます。

## Booking reply

Thanks for contacting us. Please send your preferred date, service, reference photo and any previous colour or chemical treatment. We will reply with availability and an estimate. A booking is confirmed only after we confirm the time and deposit policy.

## Why this is useful

The wording answers the questions that create back-and-forth: what to send, why the price varies, when a booking is confirmed and what happens before the appointment.
