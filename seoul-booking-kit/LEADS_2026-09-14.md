## Contact rule

Use the studio's public email, website form or listed business chat. Send one individualized message per business, keep a record, and do not send repeated messages or bulk spam. Stop after 20 qualified contacts if there are fewer than three concrete conversations or zero paid orders.

## First message (adapt the bracketed line)

> Hi, I found your studio while looking for Seoul beauty experiences for international visitors. I make short, human-reviewed English and Japanese booking replies from a studio's real menu and policies, including what guests should send, price-estimate wording and deposit/no-show information. [Mention one specific service or visitor page here.] I can send a one-page sample for your studio at no cost. Would that be useful?

Do not attach an invoice or ask for payment in the first message. If they request the pack, collect the current menu and policies first, then issue a PayPal invoice for US$79.
