# Seoul Visitor Booking Kit operations

## Offer

Sell one fixed deliverable to a small Seoul beauty studio: an English and Japanese booking pack built from its real menu and policies. Price US$79 or the KRW equivalent. Delivery within 24 hours. One factual revision.

## Sales sequence

1. Select a studio with a public menu and a visible foreign-customer path but weak or incomplete English/Japanese booking information.
2. Send the short sample request. Do not claim a revenue increase or invent a customer case.
3. If the owner asks for the pack, confirm the current menu, prices, deposit, cancellation and preparation rules.
4. Create a PayPal goods/services invoice for US$79 with a customer-specific description.
5. Start after the payment is visible in PayPal. Deliver editable text and PDF within 24 hours.

## Quality gate

- Every price, duration and policy matches the studio's source material.
- Medical or treatment claims are excluded.
- English and Japanese wording is natural and usable in a real booking conversation.
- The studio can copy each answer into its existing email, WhatsApp or booking chat.

## Stop rule

Contact 20 studios. If there are fewer than three concrete conversations or zero paid orders, change the target or offer before adding features.
